## 2. Energy and macros — the decision that overrides all 9 plans

The plans spanned 1,650–1,950 kcal. Resolution, per your locked decision:

> **Start at 1,800 kcal/day. Fallback 1,900 kcal/day if the adjustment rules in §8 trigger.**

Why 1,800 and not plan9's 1,950: plan9's TDEE of ~2,200 assumes 8–10k steps you are not yet doing. At your current ~3k steps, activity factor ≈1.40 gives TDEE ≈ **1,900–2,000** [C]. 1,800 is a modest deficit (~100–200 kcal) that permits recomp without starving training.

**Targets (all verified by script):**

| Macro | Target | Verified daily value | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | 1,800 ± 25 kcal | **1,788 kcal** (Atwater cross-check 1,766) | [V] in range |
| Protein | 126–131 g (2.0–2.2 g/kg) | **126.5 g** (2.08 g/kg; 2.63 g/kg FFM) | [V] in range |
| Fat | 30–35 g (≥0.5 g/kg floor) | **30.5 g** (0.502 g/kg) | [V] in range |
| Carbs | fill remainder | **246.2 g** | [V] |
| Fibre | ≥30 g | **30.0 g** | [V] in range |

**Known trade-off, stated plainly:** fat at 30.5 g is 15.4% of energy, below the general 20–35% guideline (plan8 flagged this). It is mathematically forced: at 1,800 kcal with 126+ g protein and 30+ g fibre, there is no room for 20% fat. All nine plans had this tension; plan5 went to 21 g (worse). 30.5 g sits inside the evidence-informed 25–35 g window from Whittaker & Wu 2021 (low fat → lower testosterone). **Do not go below 30 g.** If the 1,900 fallback triggers, fat goes to 30.8 g — the extra calories go to carbs, not fat.

**Energy availability check** [C]: (1,788 − exercise)/48 kg FFM = **33.1** on gym days (−200 kcal) and **31.4** on judo day (−280 kcal). Both above the 30 kcal/kg FFM red line from the RED-S literature. Below 30 → raise intake per §8.

### Food safety (plan6's batch rules — non-negotiable)

- Rice is a *Bacillus cereus* risk: cook, then **into the fridge within 2 h**, eat within **2 days** of cooking. That's exactly why there are 2 batch days (Sun + Wed).
- Cooked chicken: max 2 days fridge; beyond that, freezer.
- 14 freezer boxes: cook 14 days of chicken in one go if desired, but **rice never freezes-and-reheats well more than once** — rice is a every-other-day cook.
- Reheat to steaming hot (≥75 °C), once only.

### Micronutrient status (qualitative check — [E], no lab values claimed)

- **Calcium:** ~750 ml milk (≈900 mg) + 330 g broccoli (≈155 mg) ≈ **1,050 mg** vs 1,000 mg ADH — ~105%, fully covered. (The milk swap that removed egg whites closed this gap as a side effect.)
- **Vitamin D:** supplement (see §5). Delft at 52°N means essentially no cutaneous synthesis Nov–Feb — this is the strongest evidence for the D3 pill, not testosterone claims.
- **Iron/zinc:** chicken + eggs + oats + lentils cover it; vitamin C from tomato/broccoli/banana boosts non-haem iron absorption. No supplement claimed necessary — but if you ever feel persistently fatigued, ask the huisarts for ferritin/B12 rather than guessing.
- **Iodine:** iodised salt + milk cover it.
- **B12:** animal foods daily — covered.

### 4.7 Progression, loads, deloads

- **Double progression** everywhere: hit the top of the rep range on all sets at RIR 2+ → add 2.5 kg (lower) / 1–2 kg per dumbbell (upper) next session.
- **Deadlift exception:** +2.5 kg/week while ≤ RPE 8; week 4 deload = repeat week-3 load.
- **Starting loads:** week 1 is a calibration week — find the load that gives the bottom of the range at RIR 2, write it down. (plan7's specific "you can pull 50–55 kg" was an unverified claim about you; ignore it and calibrate.)
- **Weekly volume target:** 9–12 hard sets per muscle group. Chest ≈9, back ≈9–10, legs ≈12.
- **Deload:** every 6th week, or when two consecutive sessions stall at the same load — cut sets by ~40%, keep loads.
- **Log every session** (this is what the GymMane integration in the companion doc is for).

## 8. Monitoring and adjustment rules (the whole plan steers from here)

**Measure:**
1. **Weight:** daily, after toilet, before food, ~weekly same scale. Only the **14-day moving average** counts. Single days are noise (creatine water, sodium, gut content).
2. **Strength:** every session logged (load × reps × sets on the 6 anchor lifts).
3. **Judging window:** never adjust inside a 14-day window.

**Adjust (decide on day 14, 28, 42…):**

| Observation (14-day avg) | Action |
|---|---|
| Weight −0.4 to +0.4 kg AND strength flat/up | **Perfect — change nothing.** This is the recomp zone. |
| Weight down >0.5 kg | Raise to the **1,900 fallback** (§3). If already there, add another +25 g rice + 50 ml milk. |
| Weight up >0.5 kg AND strength up | Accept it — you're below maintenance, this is lean-ish gain at 60 kg. Reassess at 62.5 kg cap. |
| Weight flat but 2+ lifts stalled twice over | Check adherence (weigh the oil!), sleep, then +100 kcal via the fallback mechanism. |
| EA symptoms (fatigue, irritability, poor sleep, libido drop) or computed EA <30 | Raise intake immediately, don't wait for day 14. |
| Strength drops 2 sessions running after a deload | Extend deload a week; do not cut food. |

**Creatine caveat:** weeks 1–3 may show +1–2 kg water. Do not "correct" it by cutting food. Judge from week 4's average.

**Checkpoints:** month 3 — if available, caliper/InBody at X to verify the 21% starting estimate; month 6 and 12 — reassess timeline honestly against the 60–61 kg / 10–12% target.
